About Our Green Campus

Our college aims to provide quality education while protecting nature. We encourage students to follow eco-friendly practices and take part in environmental activities.


Our green campus promotes tree plantation, renewable energy, waste management and environmental awareness to create a cleaner and healthier future.

Solar Power in Our Campus

Solar energy is a renewable source of energy obtained from sunlight. Solar panels can be used in colleges to generate electricity and reduce dependence on conventional power.

  • Reduces electricity costs.
  • Helps reduce carbon emissions.
  • Uses clean and renewable energy.
  • Encourages sustainable development.

Waste Management

Proper waste management helps keep our campus clean and healthy.

Reduce, Reuse and Recycle

Waste management is an important part of a green campus. Students and staff can reduce waste by following proper disposal and recycling methods.


  • Separate wet and dry waste.
  • Recycle paper, plastic and other materials.
  • Use reusable bottles and bags.
  • Convert biodegradable waste into compost.
  • Avoid single-use plastics.
